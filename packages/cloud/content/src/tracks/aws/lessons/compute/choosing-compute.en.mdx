AWS has three main ways to run your code. Each is the right answer **for a different traffic shape and team**.

<ServiceCompare ids="ec2,fargate,lambda" />

## Quick decision questions

1. **Does it run longer than 15 minutes?** Yes → not Lambda.
2. **Is traffic steady 24/7, or spiky with near-zero periods?** Spiky/low → Lambda or Fargate. Steady and high → EC2/Fargate (+ Savings Plans).
3. **Does the team want to patch OSes, manage AMIs and capacity?** No → Fargate or Lambda.
4. **Need GPUs, host access, special software?** Yes → EC2.

## The cost crossover

Lambda bills **per request**; EC2/Fargate bill **per hour whether busy or not**. So Lambda is cheaper at low traffic, and the order flips at high, steady traffic. Move the slider to find the crossover:
