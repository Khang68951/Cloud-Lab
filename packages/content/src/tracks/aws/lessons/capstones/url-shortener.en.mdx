This is the classic system-design interview question — except now you can **run** your design.

## The brief

- **1,000 requests/second** on average, **3,000** at peak.
- **95% are redirects**: `GET /abc123` → `301` to the long URL.
- 5% create links: `POST /links`.
- Tiny responses (~1 KB).

## How to approach it (interview style)

1. **Clarify** — read- or write-heavy? How consistent? Do links expire?
2. **Estimate** — 1,000 rps ≈ 2.6 billion requests/month, ~130 million new links/month; ~500 bytes each → ~65 GB/month.
3. **Data model** — `code → longUrl`. Pure key-value lookups.
4. **Hot read path** — redirects must be very fast and cheap. Is a cache worth it?
5. **Resilience** — what happens when an AZ dies? When traffic triples?
6. **Cost** — which component dominates the bill? Can you trade it?

<Callout type="tip">
There is no single answer. Try **at least two designs** (e.g. containers + DynamoDB, and serverless) in a free Lab and compare the price panels before submitting.
</Callout>

After you pass, ask yourself: *at 100× the traffic, what breaks first, and what would you change?*
