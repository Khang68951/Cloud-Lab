## What CloudFront is great at

- **Latency** — served from one of 600+ edge locations near the user.
- **Origin offload** — a 90% hit ratio means your servers see 10% of reads.
- **Static and large content** — images, video, JS/CSS. Transfer from AWS origins to CloudFront is **free**.
- **Protection** — absorbs spikes; pairs with WAF and Shield.

<Callout type="warning">
**A CDN is not automatically cheaper.** CloudFront charges ~$1 per million HTTPS requests. For APIs returning **billions of tiny responses**, request fees can outweigh bandwidth savings — always check the cost tab.
</Callout>

## Hit ratio is everything

- Keep the **cache key** small: don't forward headers/cookies you don't need.
- Set `Cache-Control` properly; use **versioned file names** (`app.3f9a.js`) so long TTLs are safe.
- **Invalidations** are slow and manual — renaming files is better.

In the Lab, CloudFront has a *hit ratio* setting. Move it from 0.5 to 0.95 and watch the load on everything behind it.
