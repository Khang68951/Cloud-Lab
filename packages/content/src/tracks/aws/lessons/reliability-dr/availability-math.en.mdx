## Series multiplies; parallel multiplies the failure odds

- **Series** (a request needs A **and then** B): `A × B` — always **worse** than the weakest link.
- **Parallel** (any **one** of them is enough): `1 − (1−A) × (1−B)` — redundancy climbs fast.

## RTO, RPO and the 4 DR strategies

- **RTO** (Recovery Time Objective): **how long** until you are back.
- **RPO** (Recovery Point Objective): **how much data** (in time) you may lose.

| Strategy | RTO / RPO | Cost | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Backup & restore** | Hours / hours | $ | Only backups in another Region |
| **Pilot light** | Tens of minutes / minutes | $$ | Data replicated, servers off |
| **Warm standby** | Minutes / seconds | $$$ | A scaled-down copy running in another Region |
| **Multi-site active/active** | ~0 / ~0 | $$$$ | Full capacity in two Regions at once |

Pick by **what downtime costs the business** — not by what is fanciest.
